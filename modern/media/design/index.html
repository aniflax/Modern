Materials That Age Well

Ira Saxena · 19 June 2026

Why we specify honed limestone over polished vitrified, engineered oak over laminate, and matte ironmongery over chrome.

A finish is only as good as its fifth year. Most specification decisions in Indian residential construction optimise for the showroom, which is why so many homes look tired within two monsoons.

Stone before tile

Honed Indian limestone develops a patina; high-gloss vitrified develops scratches. One improves with traffic, the other records it.

Timber that can be repaired

Engineered oak can be sanded and re-oiled at home. Laminate cannot be repaired at all — it can only be replaced.

Specify for the fifth year and the first year takes care of itself.
