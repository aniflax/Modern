5 Reasons Indore Is Emerging as Central India's Real Estate Hotspot

Unitya Living Research · 28 July 2026

Infrastructure that arrived ahead of demand, a services economy that keeps hiring, and land still priced below comparable Tier-2 cities.

For most of the last decade, Indore was discussed as a city with potential. That framing is now out of date. The infrastructure has landed, the employment base has broadened beyond trade into IT and pharma services, and residential absorption has stayed positive through two rate cycles.

1. Infrastructure arrived before the demand

The Super Corridor, the western ring road and the metro alignment were built on projections rather than pressure. That sequencing is unusual in Indian cities and it is the single biggest reason commute times here have not deteriorated the way they have in comparable markets.

2. A hiring base that is not one industry deep

Indore's economy still rests on trade and manufacturing, but IT services, pharmaceuticals, education and food processing now employ enough people that a single sector slowdown does not empty the rental market.

3. Land pricing that still leaves room

Per-square-foot pricing in the city's better localities remains meaningfully below Pune, Jaipur and Ahmedabad for comparable specification. Buyers are not paying a premium for the story yet.

4. Buyers who ask about specification

Indore buyers are unusually well-informed and unusually patient. They ask about slab thickness and water treatment before they ask about the clubhouse, which pushes developers toward substance.

5. Liveability that has held

Nine consecutive years at the top of the national cleanliness rankings is not a marketing line; it reflects municipal capacity that shows up in daily life — collection, roads, parks, water.

The question is no longer whether Indore grows. It is whether what gets built here is worth owning in 2040.
