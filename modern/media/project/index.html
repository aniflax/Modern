Small & Minimal House On Paters on Hill

Unitya Living · 6 Aug 2026

A compact retreat shaped by light, proportion and restraint — where every square foot is considered.

Perched on the upper slope of Paters Hill, this small house was designed for a family that wanted less — less to maintain, less to heat and cool, and less between them and the landscape outside.

Design intent

The plan is a single bar, precisely placed to capture morning light in the living spaces and evening light on the terrace. Large openings are limited to the south and east; the north is solid and insulated, quiet against the hill.

Material and light

Honed limestone floors, lime-washed walls and oiled oak joinery give the house its calm. Every material was chosen for how it will look in five years, not just on handover day.

Small does not mean compromised — it means every decision has to be right.
